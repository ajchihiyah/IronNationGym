Leadership

Meet The Coach

Head Coach & Founder

Ray Marozva

Strength & Conditioning Specialist

Ray Marozva brings over six years of elite strength and conditioning expertise to Zimbabwe. As the S&C Coach for the Zimbabwe Under-20 Rugby National Team (Junior Sables) from 2023–2026, he led physical preparation across 15 international fixtures against teams from Kenya, Tunisia, Namibia, Australia, South Africa, Fiji, Uganda, and New Zealand.

He has also served as part-time S&C coach for Olympic sprinter Ngonidzashe Makusha, applying advanced periodisation, Olympic weightlifting derivatives, and force-velocity / M.A.S. training. Ray delivers programmes for tennis athletes at All Star Sports Hub and basketball athletes at Spartans Academy (UK).

Junior Sables S&C Coach ITEC Diploma (Distinction) IUSCA S&C Certified World Rugby Certified
