Program Structure

The Four Pillars of Rugby S&C

Our training methodology is built on four foundational blocks that every rugby athlete must master to perform at their peak.

Maximal Strength

Build the raw power needed for scrums, rucks, mauls, and dominant tackles. Focus on compound lifts with rugby-specific loading parameters.

Back squat & front squat progressions
Deadlift & Romanian deadlift
Bench press & overhead press
Pull-ups & weighted rows

Explosive Power

Develop the speed and explosiveness that separates good players from great ones. Plyometrics, Olympic lifts, and contrast training.

Power cleans & hang cleans
Box jumps & depth jumps
Medicine ball throws & slams
Contrast & complex training

Conditioning

Rugby is an 80-minute war. Our conditioning protocols replicate game demands with interval-based energy system development.

Repeated sprint ability (RSA)
High-intensity interval training
Tempo runs & aerobic base
Game-specific conditioning circuits
Position Specific

Forwards vs. Backs

Different positions, different demands, different programs. We do not train a scrum-half like a lock.

Forwards Program

Forwards are the engine room. They need maximal strength, anaerobic power, and the ability to repeat high-intensity efforts in contact. Our forwards program emphasizes absolute strength, neck and shoulder durability, and scrum-specific isometric strength.

Strength Focus

Absolute strength with heavy compound lifts. 3-5 rep ranges, 85-95% 1RM.

Contact Prep

Neck bridges, tackle bag work, grappling drills, and collision simulation.

Scrum Power

Isometric holds, prowler pushes, and sled drags to build scrum dominance.

Repeat Efforts

Short rest intervals between sets to simulate ruck-to-ruck demands.

Back squat: 5x5 @ 85% 1RM
Deadlift: 4x4 @ 87.5% 1RM
Bench press: 5x5 @ 82.5% 1RM
Sled push: 6x20m, 60s rest

Backs Program

Backs need speed, agility, and reactive strength. Our backs program prioritizes acceleration, change of direction, and the ability to express power at high velocities. We also build the robustness needed to survive contact.

Speed Focus

Max velocity sprinting, acceleration mechanics, and flying 10s.

Agility

Change of direction drills, reactive agility, and footwork patterns.

Reactive Strength

Depth jumps, hurdle hops, and plyometric progressions.

Contact Robustness

Hamstring prehab, neck strength, and tackling mechanics.

Power clean: 5x3 @ 75% 1RM
Box jump: 4x5, max height
Front squat: 4x6 @ 80% 1RM
10-20-10 agility: 6 reps, 90s rest

Periodization

The Annual Plan

Rugby is a year-round commitment. Our periodized approach ensures you peak when it matters most — on game day.

Off-Season
December — February

Build the Foundation

This is where champions are made. The off-season is our longest training block, focused on building maximal strength, addressing movement deficiencies, and developing aerobic capacity. Volume is high, intensity is moderate-to-high. We fix injuries, build muscle, and lay the physical groundwork for the year ahead.

Pre-Season
March — May

Sharpen the Blade

Intensity ramps up. We shift from pure strength to power and speed development. Conditioning becomes more rugby-specific with repeated sprint work and contact preparation. Position-specific demands are prioritized. By the end of pre-season, you should be physically ready to dominate from minute one.

In-Season
June — October

Maintain & Perform

Training volume drops to manage fatigue, but intensity stays high. Two strength sessions per week maintain power and prevent strength loss. Recovery protocols are dialed in. The focus shifts to game-day performance — every gym session is designed to make you better on Saturday, not tired on Saturday.

Transition
November

Recover & Rebuild

A short, active recovery phase. Light training, cross-training, and mental recovery. We assess the season, identify areas for improvement, and begin planning the next cycle. This phase prevents burnout and keeps the body resilient for the long term.

Recovery

The 4 Rs of Recovery

You do not get stronger in the gym. You get stronger when you recover. Our recovery protocols are built into every program.

01

Refuel

Post-session nutrition within 30 minutes. Protein, carbs, and hydration protocols tailored to training demands.

02

Repair

Soft tissue work, foam rolling, and targeted mobility. Our recovery suite includes massage guns and compression.

03

Restore

Sleep optimization, contrast bathing, and active recovery sessions. Sleep is the single biggest performance enhancer.

04

Regenerate

Deload weeks, load management, and injury rehabilitation. We monitor training load to prevent overtraining.
